Portuguese lesson preparation

Bring one clear goal to your first lesson.

A short brief helps a teacher spend time on the Portuguese you actually need. Use the prompts below, then keep the page nearby during the lesson.

A worked example

My starting point

I know a few greetings and can recognise some written words.

One situation I want to handle

Introducing myself to my partner’s family.

What I want feedback on

Pronunciation and a natural way to ask one follow-up question.

My next practice goal

Record a 30-second introduction and repeat the corrected version twice.
