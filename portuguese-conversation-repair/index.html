Brazilian Portuguese practice

Repair the conversation and keep going.

When a reply moves too quickly, a simple repair phrase gives you another chance to listen. Practise the request, the reply, and the next question together.

Three phrases to keep ready

Não entendi.

I did not understand.

Use it calmly, then wait for the other person to try again.

Pode repetir mais devagar?

Can you repeat that more slowly?

Ask for a slower version when you recognised the topic but missed the words.

Pode escrever, por favor?

Can you write it down, please?

Use this for a name, address, price, or destination that you want to check.

Read both sides, then change one detail

At a café

— Quanto custa?
— Doze reais.
— Não entendi. Pode repetir mais devagar?

— How much does it cost?
— Twelve reais.
— I did not understand. Can you repeat that more slowly?

Finding a place

— Onde fica o hotel?
— É ali, à esquerda.
— Pode escrever, por favor?

— Where is the hotel?
— It is over there, on the left.
— Can you write it down, please?

Making a plan

— A gente se encontra às oito.
— Não entendi. Às oito?
— Isso, às oito.

— We will meet at eight.
— I did not understand. At eight?
— Yes, at eight.

Repeat each exchange aloud. Change only one detail, such as the price, place, or time. The goal is to recover and continue, not to produce a perfect sentence on the first try.
